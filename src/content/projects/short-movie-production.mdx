### EasyCooking

EasyCooking is a thought-provoking short film that delves into the complex intersection of technology and domestic life. At its heart, the story follows a housewife whose existence has been reduced to the daily ritual of preparing meals for her husband. What appears to be a traditional domestic arrangement on the surface masks a disturbing reality of control and abuse behind closed doors.

The protagonist's life takes an unexpected turn when she discovers an innovative cooking application capable of automating every aspect of kitchen work. Through its careful exploration of domestic abuse and technological empowerment, EasyCooking presents a nuanced perspective on how seemingly simple innovations can catalyze profound personal transformation.

### Ziva

A 360° immersive short film that follows the touching story of a dog's escape from war-torn Ukraine, exploring themes of survival, resilience, and the unbreakable bond between humans and animals. This immersive experience puts viewers in the perspective of Ziva, a brave dog navigating through dangerous territories to find safety. The 360° format creates an unprecedented level of empathy and connection.

The film combines documentary-style footage with carefully crafted CGI elements to create a seamless and emotionally impactful narrative. 

### Le Chiavi dell’Ombra

Le Chiavi dell’Ombra is an immersive 360° storytelling experience that weaves a dark tale of mysticism through the historic streets of Turin. This personal project explores fictional encounters with dark magic across various locations in the city.

The 360° footage captured by the GoPro MAX was meticulously processed in DaVinci Resolve, with Fusion being utilized to create seamless magical effects that enhance the storytelling without breaking the immersive experience.
