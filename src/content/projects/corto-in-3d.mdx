In this 3D animation project, I covered the entire spectrum of production, taking on a comprehensive 360-degree role from modeling and rigging to character animation. In the showcased scenes, I animated a stylized character in a winter city setting, paying close attention to posing, facial expressions, and physical interactions with props, such as using a snow shovel and handling a jar of coins.

Beyond character acting, I was responsible for the overall set dressing to build a coherent and believable environment. Finally, to enhance the visual mood and capture the right winter atmosphere, I designed and implemented the lighting and volumetric fog effects. These elements, blending with the falling snow, provide soft lighting and deep cinematic depth to the entire scene.
