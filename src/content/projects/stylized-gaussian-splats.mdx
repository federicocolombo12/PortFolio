  Raw 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) captures look photographic from afar but reveal high-frequency "needle-cloud" covariance artifacts up close and resist stylized art direction. Inspired by Dylan Sisson's (Pixar) Houdini experiments, I wanted to see if artists could drive painterly NPR stylization over Gaussian splats <strong>natively inside the Blender 5.3 viewport</strong>—without Houdini licenses or slow CPU mesh-instancing hacks.

### Why Previous Blender Approaches Broke Down

Earlier attempts to bring 3DGS into Blender converted splats into millions of instanced quad meshes or point-cloud proxies. Pushing that geometry across the CPU-GPU bus tanked viewport interactivity below `10 FPS` and made real-time look-dev impossible.

The key unlock was **Blender 5.3 Beta's native OpenUSD `ParticleField3DGaussianSplat` primitive**. Because the point buffer stays resident on GPU, binding `StylizedSplatModifier` through Geometry Nodes exposes per-splat `scale`, `rotation`, `color`, and `opacity` attributes directly—no polygon conversion or CPU readbacks needed.

---

### Engineering Thought Process: Turning Ellipsoids into Intentional Brushstrokes

Simply randomizing splat scales turns a scene into noisy confetti. To make strokes read as deliberate brushwork, the modifier and compositor are structured in three stages:

1. **Surface-Conforming Tangent Flow (`Geometry Nodes`):** Extract normal vectors `N` from each Gaussian's covariance orientation and project a 3D curl noise field `V_curl` onto the tangent plane (`T = normalize(V_curl - dot(V_curl, N) * N)`). I then stretch the primary covariance scale (`2.5x` to `8.0x`) along `T` while flattening orthogonal axes so strokes follow the underlying curvature of the asset.
2. **Cohesive Viewport Compositing (`EEVEE-Next`):** Even stretched splats fracture along silhouettes. An 8-sector **Anisotropic Kuwahara filter** picks the lowest-variance sector color to merge overlapping brush dabs without blurring edges, followed by a Sobel luminance contour pass and a cold-press watercolor paper normal map.
3. **Artist Add-on (`stylized_splats_addon`) & Delighting R&D:** Wrapped the node parameters and compositor toggles into an N-Panel extension with 1-click presets, alongside an early delighting pass to flatten baked capture lighting before relighting.
