My role was **Lead VFX Developer & Technical Artist**, owning the audio-to-GPU interface, VR hand repulsion math, and the procedural trophy loop:

### 1. Cymatics SDF Morphing & Pitch Deadband (`VfxAudio_Interface.cs`)

Rather than spawning standard radial emitters, we baked **7 `Texture3D` Signed Distance Fields** from physical Chladni/Cymatics resonance patterns so VFX Graph could confine particle swarms to harmonic shapes.

- **Smooth Register Switching:** `VfxAudio_Interface.cs` runs continuous scale interpolation (`currentSdfScale -> targetSdfScale`) to eliminate popping when jumping octaves.
- **Acoustic Parameter Mapping:** LASP amplitude drives turbulence and brightness; normalized pitch (`80–400 Hz`) maps HSV ramps; octave index selects the active `Texture3D` SDF.
- **Pitch Deadband:** Human singing vibrato causes jitter if passed raw to shader noise. I added a thresholded deadband (`safePitchDistanceClampingTreshold`) preserving sign symmetry so steady notes stay calm while intentional bends deform the field cleanly:

```csharp
if (absDistance > safePitchDistanceClampingTreshold) {
    float over = absDistance - safePitchDistanceClampingTreshold;
    finalDistance = over * Mathf.Sign(safePitchDistance) * safePitchDistanceMultiplier;
}
vfx.SetFloat(pitchDistanceProperty, finalDistance);
```

### 2. Local-Space VR Hand Colliders (`VfxHandController.cs`)

To let players push through the particle field while the central orb rotates, tracking OpenXR `R_Palm` / `L_Palm` positions in world space broke collision alignment. Transforming both palms via `vfx.transform.InverseTransformPoint(palmPosition)` before feeding `HandPosition_R`, `HandPosition_L`, and `HandRadius` into VFX Graph kept physical repulsion locked to the simulation space.

### 3. Radial Trophy Ring (`AchievementManager.cs`, `SingleTrophyControl.cs`)

Discovered `(Texture3D, note)` harmonies unlock persistent floating pedestals arranged around the player on a `5 m` polar ring (filtered via `colorDifferenceThreshold = 0.1f` to prevent near-duplicate unlocks) using `PrimeTween` animations. Each pedestal runs its own `SingleTrophyControl.cs` proximity collider so players can disturb past vocal creations directly by hand.
