**Harmony VR: Singing The Light** is an immersive virtual reality experience that invites users to draw shapes and interact with an intimate, private environment using only the power of their voice.

In this collaborative VR project, I took charge of the visual components directly tied to voice interaction, focusing specifically on the **Particle System** and the **Achievement System**.

### Particle System
The visual core of the experience relies on multiple Particle Systems built with Unity's VFX Graph. Our main system utilizes *SDF Baked Meshes* to generate intriguing shapes derived from Cymatics audio patterns.

By using data extracted from real-time audio analysis (such as frequency and volume intensity), we manipulate the color, brightness, size, and turbulence of the particles. The primary goal was to make users feel that their creative vocal expressions could tangibly shape the space around them. Furthermore, we integrated hand colliders, allowing players to physically touch and interact with the visual effects they generated—an essential feature for a truly immersive VR experience.

### Achievement System
Beyond spontaneous creation, I developed a comprehensive achievement and reward system. As players discover new, unique voice-generated shape and color combinations, these are collected and arranged in a circular pattern around them. This long-term progression system encourages users to interact with their previous creations using their hands, making the VR world feel continuously alive, reactive, and deeply rewarding.

To explore the technical implementation of other components (such as the Audio Analysis engine or the dynamic sky shaders) and to meet the rest of the incredible team I collaborated with, please check out the full documentation on our GitHub repository!

### Avenue Cove & Politecnico di Torino
This project was developed within the prestigious framework of **Avenue Cove**, an international European initiative dedicated to rethinking and innovating education in the visual industries. Being part of this cross-border collaboration brought together diverse talents and perspectives, highlighting a strong international footprint. 

The **Politecnico di Torino** played a pivotal role as a key partner in this initiative, bridging academic excellence with cutting-edge industry standards.
