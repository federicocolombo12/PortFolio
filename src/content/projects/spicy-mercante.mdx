**Spicy Mercante** originated as a 3D top-down cozy game developed for the 2025 Fail/Safe game jam. You take on the role of a Middle Eastern merchant, dedicated to growing, grinding, and selling your own spices in a vibrant bazaar.

While the game emphasizes a relaxing atmosphere, developing it required a deep integration of **Technical Art** and **Game Development** skills, followed by a massive architectural pivot.

### 3. The 2D Pivot & Lead Programmer Role

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: '1fr 1fr', gap: '2rem', alignItems: 'center', margin: '2rem 0' }}>
  <div>
    <p>As the project was adopted by the student team <strong>Level Up Lab</strong>, the artistic direction shifted entirely to a classic 2D Pixel Art style. Since I do not have pixel art drawing skills, I transitioned out of the 3D Technical Artist role and took on the position of <strong>Lead Programmer</strong>.</p>
    <br/>
    <p>This required a massive structural refactoring of the game's architecture. I had to strip out the 3D physics and rendering pipelines, re-engineering the core mechanics, inventory systems, and UI to function seamlessly in a pure 2D environment, ensuring the codebase remained clean and scalable for the rest of the team.</p>
