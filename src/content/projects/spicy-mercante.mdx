**Spicy Mercante** is a 3D top-down cozy game developed for the 2025 Fail/Safe game jam. You take on the role of a Middle Eastern merchant, dedicated to growing, grinding, and selling your own spices in a vibrant bazaar.

While the game emphasizes a relaxing atmosphere, developing it required a deep integration of **Technical Art** and **Game Development** skills to ensure smooth gameplay and a distinctive visual identity.

### 1. Technical Art & Shaders

I was responsible for the technical 3D pipeline, from asset integration to writing custom shaders in Unity. Key implementations include:

- **Procedural Water Shader & Floating System:** I developed a custom procedural shader for the river that simulates dynamic waves and ripples using vertex displacement. To integrate this into gameplay, I engineered a physics-based floating system that reads the shader's displacement data, allowing objects to bob and react accurately to the water's surface tension in real-time.
- **Dynamic Cutaway Tool:** To maintain visibility in the top-down perspective without sacrificing environment detail, I developed a custom shader-based cutaway system. It dynamically fades out the roofs and walls of buildings when the player walks behind them, ensuring the merchant is always visible.

### 2. 3D Modeling & Asset Creation

All primary assets—including the merchant character, the marketplace stalls, and the architectural elements—were modeled and textured from scratch in Blender. The goal was to maintain a low-poly but highly expressive aesthetic that matched the game's cozy tone.
