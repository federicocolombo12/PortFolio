**"The boy and the hunter"** is a short film/showreel set in a dystopian 2058. 
It tells the story of Davide, a cynical bounty hunter marked by the loss of his family, and Alessio, a young boy caught in the vortex of crime. Their paths cross, leading them to form an unexpected bond in a world dominated by cybernetic enhancements, privatized executions, and disillusionment.
