**"The Boy and the Hunter"** is a VFX short film set in a dystopian 2058. It tells the story of Davide, a cynical bounty hunter marked by the loss of his family, and Alessio, a young boy caught in the vortex of crime. Their paths cross in a world dominated by cybernetic enhancements, privatized executions, and deep disillusionment.

For this project, my role spanned the entire pipeline, from pre-production logistics to on-set camera operation, and finally to technical art and color grading in post-production.

### 1. Pre-Production & On-Set Operations

During the initial phase, my focus was on production management and operational set organization. Managing a VFX-heavy shoot requires strict logistical planning to ensure that all practical plates are captured correctly for 3D camera tracking in post-production.

### 2. Tech Art, Shaders & Environment Dressing

In post-production, the cyberpunk aesthetic required significant environment augmentation to transform real-world locations into a dystopian future.

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: '1fr 1fr', gap: '2rem', alignItems: 'center', margin: '2rem 0' }}>
  <div>
    <p>Instead of manually placing standard 3D models, I acted as the Tech Artist, creating custom procedural shaders for the digital "dressing" of the buildings. These shaders handled neon signage, holographic advertisements, and structural decay, blending seamlessly with the live-action plates.</p>

    <p>Right before the final deadline, the city backgrounds still felt too rigid. To give the environment the required futuristic and lived-in vibe, I rapidly developed <strong>cloth simulations</strong> in Blender. Draping these massive synthetic tarps over the buildings completely changed the skyline's silhouette. Because these assets were positioned in the distance, they blended perfectly into the atmosphere and depth of field, establishing the final dystopian mood under severe time constraints.</p>
