**"The Boy and the Hunter"** is a VFX short film set in a dystopian 2058. It tells the story of Davide, a cynical bounty hunter marked by the loss of his family, and Alessio, a young boy caught in the vortex of crime. Their paths cross in a world dominated by cybernetic enhancements, privatized executions, and deep disillusionment.

For this project, my role spanned the entire pipeline, from pre-production logistics to on-set camera operation, and finally to technical art and color grading in post-production.

### 1. Pre-Production & On-Set Operations

During the initial phase, my focus was on production management and operational set organization. Managing a VFX-heavy shoot requires strict logistical planning to ensure that all practical plates are captured correctly for 3D camera tracking in post-production.

On set, I served as the **Main Camera Operator**. We shot the film using a **Sony Alpha 7III** paired with a set of **Samyang cine lenses**. To maintain consistent exposure and motion blur in outdoor daylight scenes, we heavily utilized variable ND filters, ensuring the footage remained cinematic and optimal for the subsequent CGI integration.

### 2. Tech Art & Post-Production

In post-production, my focus shifted to Technical Art and Color Grading. The cyberpunk aesthetic required significant environment augmentation to transform real-world locations into a dystopian future.

Instead of manually placing standard 3D models, I acted as the Tech Artist, creating custom procedural shaders for the digital "dressing" of the buildings. These shaders handled neon signage, holographic advertisements, and structural decay, blending seamlessly with the live-action plates. Finally, I worked as the **Colorist** in DaVinci Resolve, establishing the harsh, high-contrast, dystopian color palette.
