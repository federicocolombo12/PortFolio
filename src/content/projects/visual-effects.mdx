**"The Boy and the Hunter"** is a VFX short film set in 2058 following a bounty hunter and a boy caught up in urban cybernetic crime. My contribution spanned **Main Camera Operator** on set, **Technical Artist** for post-production augmentation in Blender, and **Colorist** in DaVinci Resolve.

### 1. On-Set Plates & Camera Operation

Shot outdoor plates as **Main Camera Operator** on a **Sony Alpha 7III** paired with **Samyang cine primes** and variable ND filters to lock shutter speed and motion blur cleanly for downstream 3D camera tracking.

### 2. Procedural Set Extensions & Cloth Silhouettes

To dress everyday urban plates into futuristic city blocks quickly, I wrote procedural facade shaders handling neon signage, holographic ads, and surface weathering—then ran rapid Blender **cloth simulations** right before delivery to drape synthetic tarps over rigid distant rooflines so they integrated naturally into depth-of-field haze.
