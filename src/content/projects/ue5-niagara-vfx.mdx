**Niagara VFX: The Magic Comet** highlights the integration of logic-driven particle systems within Unreal Engine 5 to create dynamic, interactive visual effects. The goal was to take the environment created in the first phase and transform it into a moody, fairy-tale landscape.

### 1. Anatomy of the Fairy Field
To inject magic into the environment, we engineered a dedicated Niagara System for the field itself:
- **Main Emitter:** Generates glowing orbs with a bright head and a trailing tail that changes color and shrinks based on lifespan (managed via Splines).
- **Light Renderer:** We attached a Light Renderer to the glow particles. This allowed us to dynamically control real-time lighting intensity based directly on the emissive color of the particles.
- **Wind-swept Petals:** Thousands of pink petals were introduced to drift organically with the procedural wind, completing the magical aesthetic.

### 2. Anatomy of the Meteor
The core element of the scene is the homing magic comet, split across 4 distinct Emitters for maximum fidelity:
1. **Main Emitter:** The core of the comet, the central light source from which all other elements derive.
2. **Trail Ribbon:** A module managing dynamic trails (both luminous and dark) to emphasize the sense of speed as it cuts through the air.
3. **Impact Flash:** An explosive burst of magical energy triggered exactly on the collision frame.
4. **Impact Sparks:** Secondary physical particles and sparks that scatter upon impact to give weight and volume to the explosion.

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: '1fr 1fr', gap: '2rem', alignItems: 'center', margin: '2rem 0' }}>
  <div>
    The true magic, however, lies in the **Blueprint Logic**. The Niagara system isn't isolated; we exposed a parameter (`User.TargetPosition`) inside the Emitter. A custom Blueprint calculates the *World Location* of the target Actor and injects it into Niagara every single frame using a `Set Niagara Variable (Vector3)` node. 
    <br/><br/>
    Inside the particle framework, a **Point Attraction Force** module reads this updated coordinate, dynamically bending the comet's trajectory mid-air to hunt down the target.
