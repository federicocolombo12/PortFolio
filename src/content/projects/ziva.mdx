A 360° immersive short film that follows the touching story of a dog's escape from war-torn Ukraine, exploring themes of survival, resilience, and the unbreakable bond between humans and animals. This immersive experience puts viewers in the perspective of Ziva, a brave dog navigating through dangerous territories to find safety. The 360° format creates an unprecedented level of empathy and connection.

The film combines documentary-style footage with carefully crafted CGI elements to create a seamless and emotionally impactful narrative.
