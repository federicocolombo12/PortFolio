In this 3D animation project, I took on a hybrid role as a 3D Modeler and Technical Artist, managing both the visual components and the efficiency of the production pipeline. I was responsible for asset modeling and facial rigging, developing an advanced expression system using shape keys to ensure smooth and precise performances.

<video src="https://federicocolombo12.github.io/PortFolio/projects/3D_Animation/Animation%20Stuff/Froglet/acquaGif.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline style={{float: 'left', width: '40%', margin: '0 1rem 1rem 0', borderRadius: '12px'}}></video>

To recreate the authentic stop-motion (claymation) aesthetic, I designed procedural shaders to simulate clay, animating its material imperfections in real time using drivers. I then extended this approach by creating a system to simulate water made of faux clay.

<video src="https://federicocolombo12.github.io/PortFolio/projects/3D_Animation/Animation%20Stuff/Froglet/DisplacementAndTexture.mp4" autoPlay loop muted playsInline style={{float: 'right', width: '40%', margin: '0 0 1rem 1rem', borderRadius: '12px'}}></video>

On the technical side, I provided direct support to the team's pipeline and developed a procedural tool based on Geometry Nodes for the parametric generation of vegetation and trees. This significantly optimized set dressing times and enhanced the visual quality of the environments.
