Casual ping-pong leagues fail for predictable reasons: disputes over serve rotation, bottom-of-table players quitting once Elo drops, uneven conditions (*borrowed paddle, wind*), and bored spectators waiting between rotations.

### Product & Systems Architecture

- **Dual Ladder & Contextual Elo (`elo.ts`):** Combines an absolute Elo ladder with a seasonal ATP Race (rewarding volume and close games so trailing players stay active) plus contextual multipliers (`wind`, `beer`, `dark`, `derby`, `borrowed_racket`), a `+15%` activity rhythm bonus, and weekly `-5` decay past 7 idle days (floored at `1200`).
- **One-Handed Live Referee & Firestore Sync (`App.tsx`, `EloContext.tsx`):** Tracks serve turns, deuce states (`10-10+`), and 1-tap match telemetry (*Smash, Ace, Edge, Net*) synced via `onSnapshot` across spectator clients, plus automatic spectator eviction and anti-IDOR server sanitization (`security.ts`).
- **Markov Backward-Induction Live Odds (`betting.ts`):** Evaluates exact win probability `dp[score1][score2]` point-by-point during rallies (anchored by closed-form deuce geometric series `deuceProb = (p * p) / (p * p + q * q)`) across 5 virtual-coin markets with Anti-Biscotto rules locking active players to their own side:

```ts
const p = Math.min(0.8, Math.max(0.2, 1 / (1 + Math.pow(10, (p2Elo - p1Elo) / 400))));
const deuceProb = (p * p) / (p * p + (1 - p) * (1 - p));
for (let i = targetPoints; i >= score1; i--) {
  for (let j = targetPoints; j >= score2; j--) {
    dp[i][j] = (i >= targetPoints - 1 && j >= targetPoints - 1 && i === j)
      ? deuceProb : p * dp[i + 1][j] + (1 - p) * dp[i][j + 1];
  }
}
```

- **Zero-Asset Web Audio SFX (`soundEffects.ts`):** Synthesizes match SFX and unlockable cosmetic victory jingles procedurally via `AudioContext` oscillators/envelopes (**0 KB** audio bundle overhead).
- **Verification:** Strict `tsc --noEmit` check and **22/22 unit assertions** (`npm test`) covering Elo multipliers, decay, DP convergence, Anti-Biscotto, and Anti-IDOR sanitization.
