Le Chiavi dell’Ombra is an immersive 360° storytelling experience that weaves a dark tale of mysticism through the historic streets of Turin. This personal project explores fictional encounters with dark magic across various locations in the city.

The 360° footage captured by the GoPro MAX was meticulously processed in DaVinci Resolve, with Fusion being utilized to create seamless magical effects that enhance the storytelling without breaking the immersive experience.
