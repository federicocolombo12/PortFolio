Placing hanging cables manually with Bezier curves while building urban/sci-fi levels wastes hours and breaks completely whenever modular building kits get shifted around. I built **Smart Cable Generator** (`bpy` add-on) to make sagging cable bundles fast and non-destructive.

### Iteration 1: Surface Crawler vs. Modular Reality

My first prototype ran a custom **Dijkstra shortest-path solver** over `bmesh` topology so cables would hug building geometry between two selected vertices.

In real modular environments, that broke immediately: environment kits consist of disconnected meshes, so trying to wire an antenna on Building A to an AC unit on Building B leaves no continuous edge graph to traverse. I threw out the surface crawler and switched to world-space coordinates (`matrix_world @ v.co`) to span open air between arbitrary separate objects.

### Iteration 2: Distance-Proportional Parabolic Sag

To avoid physics baking overhead, cables evaluate a lightweight parabolic curve `4.0 * gravity_sag * t * (1.0 - t)`.

Testing across props and facades quickly showed that a fixed meter drop looked right on a 2m doorway but vanished across a 40m street gap. Scaling sag proportionally to endpoint span (`distance = (p1 - p2).length`) keeps slack visually consistent across any asset scale.

### Iteration 3: Game-Engine Export Panel (V2.0)

<img src={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}/projects/pipeline-tools/ui_screenshot.jpg`} alt="Smart Cable Generator 2.0 UI Panel" style={{width: '100%', borderRadius: '12px', boxShadow: '0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)', margin: '2rem 0'}} />

Wrapped the script into a Blender N-Panel tool ready for UE5/Unity workflows:
1. **Organic Bundles:** `Cable Count` spawns grouped lines with seeded X/Y endpoint jitter and sag variation instead of sterile parallel tubes.
2. **Material Picker:** Assigns a rubber/cable shader across all generated splines at creation time.
3. **Auto-Convert for FBX:** Optional toggle converts Blender curve splines straight into clean polygon meshes ready for immediate FBX export.
