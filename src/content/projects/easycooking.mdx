EasyCooking is a thought-provoking short film that delves into the complex intersection of technology and domestic life. At its heart, the story follows a housewife whose existence has been reduced to the daily ritual of preparing meals for her husband. What appears to be a traditional domestic arrangement on the surface masks a disturbing reality of control and abuse behind closed doors.

The protagonist's life takes an unexpected turn when she discovers an innovative cooking application capable of automating every aspect of kitchen work. Through its careful exploration of domestic abuse and technological empowerment, EasyCooking presents a nuanced perspective on how seemingly simple innovations can catalyze profound personal transformation.
