## 🧭 The Research Pivot: From Environmental Reconstruction to Bio-Adaptive Implicit Fields

The thesis originally began with an exploration of **Real-time Environmental Mesh Reconstruction** on standalone Mixed Reality hardware (Meta Quest 3). Early prototypes investigated raw depth buffer extraction via Meta's Depth API, camera reprojection, and real-time mesh meshing algorithms.

However, during early R&D, open-source frameworks such as `QuestRoomScan` and `UnityGaussianSplatting` matured, offering standardized out-of-the-box environmental scanning solutions. Recognizing that reproducing existing environmental mesh reconstruction pipelines would yield limited research novelty, I made a pivotal strategic decision: **shift the focus to Bio-Adaptive Implicit Representations via Pure Mathematical Signed Distance Fields (SDFs)**.

Instead of capturing static real-world meshes, the project shifted toward creating **living, infinite-resolution procedural geometries driven by continuous human physiology**. As this pipeline expanded to support multi-sensor hardware (BLE ECG, pulse oximeters, serial microcontrollers) and required guaranteed 90 Hz framerates on standalone mobile VR headsets, the architecture crystallized into **PulseEngine**.

## 🎛️ 1. Virtual DAW Mixer Console (`DAWMixerWindow`)

Handling continuous telemetry streams (OSC, WebSockets, Serial, Bluetooth Low Energy) often results in chaotic spaghetti code across GameObjects. PulseEngine solves this by adopting proven paradigms from Digital Audio Workstations (Ableton Live, Logic Pro):

* **Hardware-Style Channel Strips:** Each telemetry signal (e.g. `heart_rate`, `spo2`, `hrv_rmssd`, `respiration`) is mapped to a dedicated channel strip with interactive faders, gain calibration, smoothing filters, and real-time OLED oscilloscopes with VU meters.
* **Engine-Level Mute [M] & Solo [S]:** Allows clinicians and technical artists to isolate specific physiological drivers or mute erratic sensor readings, gracefully restoring restful baselines without terminating the network socket.
* **Inbound Signal Sniffer & Auto-Discovery:** Automatically scans incoming telemetry packets, identifies unmapped keys, and provisions pre-configured channel strips in a single click.
* **1-Click Preset Snapshots:** Serializes complete session configurations (channels, faders, node hierarchies, materials, gradients) into ScriptableObjects for instant recall during clinical trials.

## 🧬 2. Volumetric SDF Engine & CSG Architecture

Signed Distance Fields are evaluated entirely on the GPU without polygonal meshes, delivering infinite surface resolution and continuous topological deformations:

* **Order-Independent CSG Hierarchy (MudBun-Inspired):** Sequential top-to-bottom accumulation via Unity sibling index. Supports Smooth Union, Smooth Subtraction, and Smooth Intersection with per-node `blendSoftness` modulation.
* **Standalone Modifier Volumes (`SdfModifierVolume`):** Autonomous force-field deformers (Twist, Bend, Bounded 3D Noise) featuring double-wireframe bounds (inner core and smooth falloff margins) that deform overlapping geometric nodes in metric space.
* **Object-Space Triplanar Mapping:** Because mathematical distance fields lack polygonal UVs, surface textures are projected seamlessly along local Cartesian axes ($X, Y, Z$) with exponential weight normalization: `w_i = max(0.0, |N_i| - 0.2)⁴` where `ŵ_i = w_i / (w_x + w_y + w_z)`.
* **Morten Mikkelsen Surface Gradients:** Standard triplanar normal maps produce jarring 180° seams and C¹ discontinuities along symmetry planes ($X=0, Y=0, Z=0$). PulseEngine implements Morten Mikkelsen's surface gradient formulation: `∇_surf(h) = ∇h - (N · ∇h)N`, ensuring continuous, seam-free micro-relief across dynamic organic surfaces.
* **View-Space MatCap Shading & URP Lighting:** Real-time cellular, bone, and flesh shading modes with direct binding to Unity Universal Render Pipeline (URP) main light directional vectors.
* **Live Scene View Sculpting:** Full `[ExecuteAlways]` support allowing real-time editing with native Unity transform gizmos without entering Play Mode.

## ⚡ 3. GPU Voxelizer & VFX Graph Pipeline

Evaluating full-screen solid raymarching in VR at $2064 \times 2208$ pixels per eye creates severe GPU fill-rate bottlenecks. To solve this, PulseEngine integrates a high-performance **GPU Voxelizer** (`SdfVolumeVoxelizer`) acting as a bridge to **Unity Visual Effect Graph**:

* **Sub-Millisecond 3D Texture Generation:** A Compute Shader evaluates the entire CSG hierarchy and modifier stack over a 3D thread group grid ($8 \times 8 \times 8$ threads per group), writing an `RGBAHalf` 3D RenderTexture at $128^3$ resolution in under **0.8 ms**.
  * **Red (R) Channel:** Signed distance value in normalized metric units, consumed by VFX Graph for surface attraction, conformity, and collision.
  * **Green, Blue, Alpha (GBA) Channels:** Interpolated RGB surface color for dynamic cellular tinting.
* **Physics-Only Mode (Fill-Rate Decoupling):** On mobile XR hardware (Meta Quest 3), the solid fragment raymarching pass can be disabled entirely. The SDF continues to be voxelized into the 3D texture, allowing millions of particles to adhere, collide, and dance across the invisible mathematical surface with **zero screen fragment raymarching cost**.

## 🫀 4. Clinical Biofeedback Suite (Flagship Showcase)

In psychotherapy and trauma therapy, patients frequently struggle with **interoception**—the ability to perceive and interpret internal autonomic states. 

PulseEngine provides a visceral perceptual feedback medium by translating live physiological biomarkers into the morphology, texture, and particle physics of a responsive virtual organism across 3 calibrated clinical states:

* **Heart Rate (BPM):** Drives the rhythm of the `DataStreamMetronome`, triggering physical systolic pulse contractions on each cardiac cycle.
* **Blood Oxygenation (SpO₂):** Modulates cellular saturation; hypoxia induces cyanotic deep-indigo hues and cellular desaturation.
* **Autonomic Balance (HRV RMSSD):** Gold standard indicator of parasympathetic vagal tone. High RMSSD (calm) increases CSG blend softness (0.45 → 0.65), melting shapes into harmonic fluid spheres. Suppressed RMSSD (acute stress) reduces blend softness (0.05), sharpens geometry, and triggers chaotic particle turbulence.
