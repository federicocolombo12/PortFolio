### Awaken Asylum

This psychological narrative experience takes place in an abandoned asylum, where players piece together fragments of memory through environmental storytelling. Beginning in a desolate room, you'll explore dimly lit corridors and interact with historically accurate objects that trigger vivid flashbacks to the institution's active period. Each discovered item reveals a new layer of the asylum's history and your own mysterious connection to it.

This experience is based on extensive research into Piedmont's psychiatric institutions and the Basaglia Law of 1978, which revolutionized mental health care in Italy. 

### Line Operator

**Line Operator** is a gripping 3D point-and-click adventure set in the heart of the Cold War, where silence and secrecy reign supreme. You step into the role of a communications officer stationed in a dimly lit control room, surrounded by towering file cabinets, cryptic devices, and the hum of hidden machinery. Your mission is to encode and transmit classified messages, ensuring vital information reaches its destination before time runs out.

The room holds three enigmatic machines, each presenting a unique challenge: the Switch Machine, the Cipher Machine, and the Newton’s Pendulum.

### Nihil

**Nihil** is an action-adventure prototype developed during the Game Design course at Politecnico di Torino. Set in a corrupted world ruled by the goddess Achlys, the player controls a shapeshifter with three distinct forms (human, agile, strong), each offering unique abilities for combat, traversal, and environmental puzzles. 

My contribution focused on the development side: I implemented the gameplay mechanics, enemy AI, and all Unity coding aspects, ensuring the transformation system, dynamic combat interactions, and environmental logic worked seamlessly together.

### Spicy Mercante

**Spicy Mercante** is a 3D top view cozy game. In it, you take on the role of a Middle Eastern merchant, dedicated to growing, grinding, and selling your own spices in a vibrant bazaar.

In this game I handled the 3D part, writing shaders, developing tools like the cutaway tool that lets you see through buildings and a handy river tool with waves and custom physics, I also created and textured most of the 3D models in it. The game was made in the 2025 Fail/Safe game jam.
