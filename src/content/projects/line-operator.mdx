**Line Operator** is an early student/jam 3D point-and-click puzzle game set inside a Cold War communications control room, centered around decoding and transmitting timed messages across three tactile desktop mechanisms: the Switch Machine, the Cipher Machine, and Newton's Pendulum.

My main contribution was **3D asset modeling and pipeline integration** across almost all environment props and control-room devices (built when I was first learning Blender-to-Unity asset workflows), alongside Unity scene assembly and C# interaction wiring.
