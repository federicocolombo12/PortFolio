Early CG generalist environment study in **Blender (Cycles)** covering hard-surface modeling, UV unwrapping, procedural PBR surface wear, and multi-camera interior lighting across a retro arcade and racing simulator room.

- **Scope:** Full scene modeling, Shader Editor PBR layering (combining image textures with procedural roughness/dust breakup), HDRI + practical 3-point neon lighting, and Cycles compositor passes (glare, subtle chromatic aberration).
