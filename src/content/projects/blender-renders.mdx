For this Computer Graphics project, I was tasked with creating high-quality, photorealistic 3D renders entirely from scratch. I managed the complete CGI (Computer-Generated Imagery) pipeline, taking the project from initial concept and blockout to the final rendered composition.

- **Role:** 3D Artist / CG Generalist
- **Tech Stack & Tools:** Blender, Cycles Render Engine, PBR Material Workflow, Node-based Procedural Texturing.

---

## 🛠️ Key Activities & CGI Pipeline

### 1. 3D Modeling & Topology
I built the geometry of the scene focusing on clean topology and proper edge flow to ensure realistic light reflection and smooth subdivision surfaces.
- **Hard-Surface & Organic Modeling:** Utilized a mix of polygonal modeling techniques, boolean operations, and subdivision surface modifiers to create detailed and accurate assets.
- **Scene Optimization:** Managed polygon counts and utilized instancing to keep the viewport responsive and optimize rendering times without sacrificing visual quality.

### 2. UV Unwrapping & Texturing (PBR)
To achieve photorealism, I applied a Physically Based Rendering (PBR) workflow to all assets in the scene.
- **UV Mapping:** Carefully unwrapped 3D models to minimize texture stretching and hide seams in less visible areas.
- **Material Creation:** Built complex node trees in Blender's Shader Editor. I combined image textures (Albedo, Roughness, Normal, and Displacement maps) with procedural nodes to create realistic surface imperfections like dust, scratches, and wear-and-tear.

### 3. Lighting & Camera Setup
Lighting was a crucial step to set the mood and highlight the geometry of the models.
- **Illumination Setup:** Combined HDRI (High Dynamic Range Imaging) for realistic environmental reflections with a customized 3-point lighting setup (Key, Fill, and Back/Rim lights) to enhance depth and volume.
- **Cinematic Composition:** Set up virtual cameras using real-world photographic principles, adjusting focal length, depth of field (DoF), and rule-of-thirds composition to guide the viewer's eye.

### 4. Rendering & Post-Processing
- **Cycles Engine:** Configured the Cycles path-tracing engine for the final output, optimizing sample counts and light bounces for a noise-free image.
- **Compositing:** Used Blender’s built-in Compositor to add post-processing effects such as glare, color grading, and subtle chromatic aberration, giving the final renders a cinematic and polished look.
